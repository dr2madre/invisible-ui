A carousel with three modes: `slide` (full-bleed background slides with an
overlay), `gallery` (a scrolling row of cards), and `coverflow` — a 3D
"jukebox" where the active item is centered and its neighbors recede (set
`orientation="vertical"` to stack the flow top-to-bottom). Render custom slide
content with the `children` snippet (`{ item, index, active }`).

### Import

<ImportSnippet component="carousel" />

In Elements:

```html
<ds-carousel label="Featured photos" loop>
  <article>First slide</article>
  <article>Second slide</article>
  <article>Third slide</article>
</ds-carousel>
```

Each child becomes a slide. Without children, set the `items` property to
draw the built-in slides. The element sends `change` with `detail.index`.

### Props

<PropsTable component="carousel" />

Each slide's `image` is rendered as given: pass only URLs you trust or have
sanitized. See [Security](/invisible-ui/api/#security).

### Accessibility

- Labelled region with previous/next controls and indicator buttons; the active
  slide is tracked for assistive tech. The role names, the "N of M" slide names
  and the indicator names come from the message catalog.
- Coverflow neighbours are scaled and turned, never faded, so their text keeps
  its contrast; slides more than two places away are hidden.
- The built-in slide's title sits on a dark overlay that stays at least 55%
  black. White text keeps 4.76:1 or better over the lightest image (measured
  against a white image: 4.76:1 at 55%, 8.52:1 at the 70% base).
- The slide track, the coverflow and the gallery scroll do not animate under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- In right-to-left text Left and Right swap, so the arrows keep following the reading direction; the page's `dir` is read when a key is pressed, with or without a locale provider. The previous and next arrows are mirrored, the slide track moves the other way, and the coverflow places the next slide on the left.

### Example

```svelte
<Carousel label="Photos" items={[{ image: "/a.jpg", title: "A" }]} />
```

In React, `renderItem` replaces the `children` snippet, and `index` with
`onIndexChange` makes the current slide controlled:

```tsx
<Carousel
  label="Albums"
  variant="gallery"
  items={albums}
  index={current}
  onIndexChange={setCurrent}
  renderItem={({ item, active }) => <Card title={String(item.title)} surface={active ? "default" : "secondary"} />}
/>
```

Themeable via `--ds-carousel-*`.
