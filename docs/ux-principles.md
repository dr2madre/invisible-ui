# UX principles

Product and interaction principles for Invisible UI. They guide behavior and
copy decisions across components, alongside
[`foundations.md`](./foundations.md) (visual defaults) and
[`copy-guidelines.md`](./copy-guidelines.md) (writing style).

- **Task completion first.** Clarity and native platform expectations
  outrank decoration.
- **Buttons name outcomes.** Controls expose clear labels, feedback, and a
  way to recover from a mistake.
- **Preserve what exists.** Keep the incumbent information architecture,
  behavior, copy, and visual identity unless a redesign or product change is
  explicitly requested.
- **No invented claims.** Never state testimonials, metrics, pricing,
  customers, or future guarantees that aren't real.
- **Record significant decisions.** A significant interaction or
  architecture change gets an entry in [`adr/`](./adr/) when the decision is
  worth preserving.
